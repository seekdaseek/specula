# specula — pricing model input (14 Nov 2026, Phase 1b)

No billing code exists. This file turns `COSTS.md` plus today's measurements into a cost model and a proposed tier ladder.
Numbers are generated by `phase0/pricing.py` (output kept in `phase0/pricing-output.md`). Every input and its source is in `COSTS.md` section 7.
Month = 30 days. A "customer" is one monitored program. Marginal cost = credits x Helius's $5 per million; the first 10M credits of a Developer plan are already paid for by the $49 fee.

## 1. What one program costs per month (design A: webhook + 5-minute reconciler)

Credits per program per month = tx/month (1 credit per webhook event) + 17,280 reconciler `getSignaturesForAddress` calls (program id + programdata account, every 5 min) + 1.1% of tx/month re-fetched by the reconciler (the webhook missed 3 of 273 in the live run, all in the first minute; see section 4).

| program | tx/day (measured today) | tx/month | credits/month | marginal $/month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| auction-house | 2 | 60 | 17,341 | $0.09 |
| squads-v3 | 41 | 1,230 | 18,524 | $0.09 |
| jupiter-lend | 6,076 | 182,280 | 201,565 | $1.01 |
| squads-v4 | 6,214 | 186,420 | 205,751 | $1.03 |
| kamino-lend (**extrapolated**: 20,000 signatures spanned 22,791 s) | 75,819 | 2,274,570 | 2,316,870 | $11.58 |
| raydium-clmm (**extrapolated** from a 74 s window, order of magnitude only) | 3,502,703 | 105,081,090 | 106,254,262 | $531.27 |

Median of the six: (6,076 + 6,214) / 2 = 6,145 tx/day, about $1.02 per program per month in credits. The reconciler is a small fixed floor: 17,280 credits = $0.09.

## 2. Fair share of the Helius plan fee

All N customers on one tier, cheapest Helius plan including $5/M overage (Developer $49 / 10M credits, Business $499 / 100M, Professional $999 / 200M). VPS cost is an **assumption of $10/month (UNVERIFIED, replace with the real Hetzner price)**.

| tier | N=10 | N=50 | N=200 |
|---|---|---|---|
| auction-house / squads-v3 | $4.90 (+VPS $1.00) | $0.98 (+$0.20) | $0.24 (+$0.05) |
| jupiter-lend | $4.90 (+$1.00) | $0.99 (+$0.20) | $1.00 (+$0.05) |
| squads-v4 | $4.90 (+$1.00) | $1.01 (+$0.20) | $1.02 (+$0.05) |
| kamino-lend (extrap) | $11.48 (+$1.00) | $11.56 Business (+$0.20) | $11.58 Business (+$0.05) |
| raydium-clmm (extrap) | $531.17 Business (+$1.00) | $531.25 (+$0.20) | $531.27 (+$0.05) |

Reading it: below ~1M credits per customer the plan fee dominates (10 customers: $4.90 each); above that the credit rate dominates and the plan fee vanishes into it. A Kamino-sized program costs about 12x a Jupiter-Lend-sized one at any customer count, and a Raydium-sized one is not a customer shape on a flat price.

## 3. Proposed tier ladder (keyed on monthly tx volume)

These prices are **proposals, not measurements, and demand is unvalidated.** They keep a Kamino-sized program off the cheap tier because each tier has a transaction cap and a metered overage.

| tier | tx/month included | price/month | worst-case Helius cost at the cap | margin at cap, before fixed costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 100,000 (~3.3k/day) | $9 | $0.59 | $8.41 |
| Growth | 1,000,000 (~33k/day) | $39 | $5.14 | $33.86 |
| Scale | 5,000,000 (~166k/day) | $149 | $25.36 | $123.64 |
| Custom | above 5,000,000 | quote: at least 3x credit cost ($15 per million tx) with a $499 minimum | n/a | n/a |

Overage above a cap: **$1.50 per 100k tx** (3x the $0.50 credit cost). Webhook events are billed by Helius whether or not we accept them (COSTS.md), so the cap cannot be enforced by dropping traffic; it has to be enforced by pausing the program's alerts or by moving the customer up a tier.

Where today's six land: auction-house and squads-v3 on Starter; jupiter-lend and squads-v4 (about 185k tx/month) on Growth; kamino-lend (about 2.3M) on Scale; raydium-clmm (about 105M) on Custom only.

## 4. Break-even customer count

Fixed costs: Helius Developer $49 + VPS $10 (assumed) = $59/month. Break-even = fixed / (price - marginal cost per customer).

| mix | marginal cost/customer | break-even |
|---|---|---|
| Starter at its cap, $9 | $0.59 | **8** customers |
| Starter, typical load (auction-house), $9 | $0.09 | 7 |
| Growth at its cap, $39 | $5.14 | **2** customers |
| Growth, typical load (jupiter-lend), $39 | $1.01 | 2 |

Conservative: it charges marginal credits at $5/M even though a Developer plan includes 10M credits. With Starter-only customers the business needs 8 paying customers to cover its infrastructure; that says nothing about whether 8 teams exist who would pay $9. Revenue-side demand is an open risk (TRACKER.md).

## 5. Measured on 14 Nov 2026 (live Helius webhook, 60.1 min, Mac)

From the database, not logs (`scripts/live-report.ts`, window 08:54:10Z to 09:54:00Z, Jupiter Lend + Squads v4):

- 273 distinct signatures: **270 seen by both**, **0 by webhook only**, **3 by poll only**. The 3 poll-only transactions have block times 23 to 54 s before the first webhook delivery arrived (08:55:17Z), so the webhook became active about one minute after creation; from the first delivery on, 270 of 270 arrived (0 missed).
- Webhook latency, receive time minus block time: **p50 1.2 s, p95 1.8 s, max 2.8 s** (n=270). Block time has 1 s resolution, so read this as +-1 s. Poll latency for the same signatures: p50 159 s, p95 290 s (5-minute cadence).
- **Failed transactions do arrive by webhook**: 7 failed transactions in the window, all 7 delivered (and all 7 seen by the poller).
- Credits, derived from our own call counts (`scripts/live-credits.ts`): 797 = 52 getSignaturesForAddress + 273 getTransaction + 2 getAccountInfo + 270 webhook events + 200 for webhook create/delete via API. **The Helius dashboard figure was not read (UNVERIFIED)**; the 273 getTransaction calls are the old reconciler re-fetching what the webhook had already delivered, which the current code no longer does.

## 6. UNVERIFIED / assumptions

- VPS price ($10/month) is an assumption.
- Helius plan fee, included credits, overage rate and the 1-credit webhook event are the values fetched on 14 Nov 2026 (COSTS.md); not re-fetched today and not checked against an invoice or the dashboard.
- The 1.1% webhook miss rate is 3 of 273 over one hour, all inside the first minute after webhook creation; steady-state miss rate is 0 of 270 and could be higher over days. The reconciler exists for that case.
- Kamino and Raydium traffic are extrapolations.
- Proposed prices, caps and overage are not tested against any buyer.
